Carlisle Companies (NYSE: CSL) – Initiating Coverage

Consortium Research Group · Industrials / Building Materials · July 8, 2025

Rating: Overweight   Price target: $526   Price at publication: $400.66 (about 31% upside)

I wrote this initiation as an analyst on the Consortium Research Group's Industrials team. The report is titled "The Multi-Billion Dollar Bet on The Roofing Industry."

Thesis

  • A deliberate transformation: over seven years Carlisle sold four segments, including Carlisle Interconnect Technologies to Amphenol for about $2B, to become a focused building envelope business. Revenue grew 33% over eight years while earnings from continuing operations grew 154%.
  • Two core segments: Carlisle Construction Materials (CCM) is a leader in commercial roofing, and Carlisle Weatherproofing Technologies (CWT) is the higher-growth segment adding air and vapor barriers, waterproofing, coatings, and sealants.
  • Capital return: about $3.2B of share repurchases over two years under the Vision 2030 plan, which supports per-share earnings growth.
  • Roofing demand: reroofing is hard to defer, and the industry is entering the replacement cycle for roofs from the mid-2000s construction boom.

Valuation

The $526 target is based on a discounted cash flow model, which implies about 31% upside to the price at publication.
