Diversified Energy (DEC) Deep Dive

A multi-part fundamental study of Diversified Energy Company, an acquirer of mature natural gas wells.

  • Compared asset retirement obligations (ARO) per well against peers
  • Studied derivative accounting and operating cash flow dilution history
  • Analyzed PV-10 reserve valuation and the NSAI reserve report
  • Modeled the Maverick Natural Resources acquisition pro forma and the Camino acquisition, including the Carlyle/DEC joint venture structure
  • Built a NAV model in Excel, updated to Q1 2026, with an automated Python workflow
