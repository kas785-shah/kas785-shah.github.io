Rivian (NASDAQ: RIVN) – Short Pitch

Written November 19, 2025

Entry: $14.72 per share ($18.05B market cap)   12-month price target: $11 (about 26% downside)   24-month price target: $8

The pitch argues that Rivian's R2 growth story assumes mass-market volume and healthy margins at the same time, while the Street overlooks management credibility, governance, and liquidity risks.

Three-part thesis

  • Volume or margin, not both: consensus implies Rivian captures 40%+ of a roughly 600K-unit premium crossover market. R2's per-unit cost of about $50K is above its $45K base price, so profit requires pricier trims that shrink the addressable market.
  • Management and governance: the work covers a $250M IPO-related settlement tied to pricing, recurring lawsuits, about $12.7M of insider selling by the CEO and CFO with no purchases, and revenue recognition of VW joint venture funding that flatters gross profit.
  • Capex and liquidity: about $7B of R2-related capex would need roughly 31% gross margins to break even, against 15-20% guided. I estimated about 44 months of runway and a possible capital raise within 12-18 months if R2 is not contribution-margin positive immediately.

Valuation

Rivian traded at about 2.9x EV/NTM sales versus a historical average near 1.7x. The price targets apply multiples to revenue built bottom-up from vehicle ASP and delivery volume, split into automotive and software/services.
