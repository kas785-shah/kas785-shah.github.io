Tutor Perini Corp (NYSE: TPC) – Initiating Coverage

Consortium Research Group · Industrials / Construction · August 24, 2025

Rating: Equal Weight   Price target: $59   Price at publication: $58.43 (about 1% upside)

I wrote this initiation as an analyst on the Consortium Research Group's Industrials team. TPC had risen about 100% since May 2025, so the question was how much of the good news was already in the price.

Thesis

  • Record backlog: a $21.1B backlog, roughly 5x 2024 revenue of $4.3B and mostly civil contracts from state and local governments, gives unusual visibility on revenue.
  • Turning profitable: Q1 2025 brought guidance for positive net income for the first time since 2021, plus the $1.8B NYC Midtown Bus Terminal win. Management expects EPS to double in 2026 and 2027 as backlog converts.
  • Margin expansion: civil segment margins have improved from about 8% to above 12%, which drives most of the EPS growth.

Valuation

The $59 target comes from a DCF with multiple scenario cases, cross-checked against comparable companies. Because the stock had already re-rated, the upside was limited, which is why the rating is Equal Weight.

Risks to the rating

Executing so many large projects at once, newer projects carrying lower margins than the Street expects, and the pace of litigation resolution.
